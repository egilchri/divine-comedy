Paradiso * Canto II

Dante and Beatrice ascend from the Earthly Paradise and are drawn with instantaneous speed into the first celestial sphere, the Moon. They physically enter its substance, which Dante describes as a solid, luminous pearl that encloses them without parting. Inside the Moon, Dante observes the dark spots visible from Earth and asks Beatrice for a physical explanation. He posits his own theory that the spots are caused by variations in the density of the lunar matter. Beatrice refutes his hypothesis, first using a thought experiment involving three mirrors to demonstrate flaws in his reasoning about reflected light. She then explains that the markings are not caused by physical density but by the varying degrees of "formal principle," or celestial virtue, that different parts of the Moon receive from the heavens. This difference in virtue results in varying degrees of luminosity.

Paradiso • Canto II

O voi che siete in piccioletta barca,
desiderosi d’ascoltar, seguiti
dietro al mio legno che cantando varca,

Oh, you who are in a tiny boat, eager to listen, follow behind my ship that cuts through the waves, singing.

tornate a riveder li vostri liti:
non vi mettete in pelago, ché forse,
perdendo me, rimarreste smarriti.

Return to see your shores again; do not venture out to sea, for perhaps, losing me, you would remain lost.

L’acqua ch’io prendo già mai non si corse;
Minerva spira, e conducemi Appollo,
e nove Muse mi dimostran l’Orse.

The water I drink has never been navigated; Minerva breathes, and Apollo leads me, and the nine Muses show me the Bears.

Voialtri pochi che drizzaste il collo
per tempo al pan de li angeli, del quale
vivesi qui ma non sen vien satollo,

You few who raised your necksearly to the bread of angels, whichis eaten here, but never satisfies,

metter potete ben per l’alto sale
vostro navigio, servando mio solco
dinanzi a l’acqua che ritorna equale.

Better you may fare for the deeper seayour vessel, following my wakeahead of the water that returns the same.

Que’ glorïosi che passaro al Colco
non s’ammiraron come voi farete,
quando Iasón vider fatto bifolco.

Than those glorious ones who passed to Colchisdid not marvel as you will,when they saw Jason become a plowman.

La concreata e perpetüa sete
del deïforme regno cen portava
veloci quasi come ’l ciel vedete.

The inborn and perpetual thirstfor the divine realm bore us onward,swiftly, almost as the heavens, you see.

Beatrice in suso, e io in lei guardava;
e forse in tanto in quanto un quadrel posa
e vola e da la noce si dischiava,

Beatrice above, and I looked up at her; and perhaps in the time it takes an arrow to settle, and fly, and detach from its notch,

giunto mi vidi ove mirabil cosa
mi torse il viso a sé; e però quella
cui non potea mia cura essere ascosa,

I found myself arrived where a marvelous thing turned my gaze towards itself; and therefore she, to whom my care could not be hidden,

volta ver’ me, sì lieta come bella,
«Drizza la mente in Dio grata», mi disse,
«che n’ha congiunti con la prima stella».

turned toward me, as happy as she was beautiful, "Turn your mind to God gratefully," she said to me, "for He has joined us with the first star."

Parev’ a me che nube ne coprisse
lucida, spessa, solida e pulita,
quasi adamante che lo sol ferisse.

I saw a cloud cover us,bright, thick, solid, and clean,almost like a diamond struck by the sun.

Per entro sé l’etterna margarita
ne ricevette, com’ acqua recepe
raggio di luce permanendo unita.

Within itself the eternal pearl received him, as water receives a ray of light, remaining whole.

S’io era corpo, e qui non si concepe
com’ una dimensione altra patio,
ch’esser convien se corpo in corpo repe,

If I were a body, and it cannot be conceived how one dimension could endure another, which must be the case if a body penetrates a body,

accender ne dovria più il disio
di veder quella essenza in che si vede
come nostra natura e Dio s’unio.

more would I feel the burning urgeto see that essence wherein is revealedhow our nature and God became one.

Lì si vedrà ciò che tenem per fede,
non dimostrato, ma fia per sé noto
a guisa del ver primo che l’uom crede.

There, one shall see that which we hold as faith,unproven, but revealed, known of itself,as the first truth that humankind believes.

Io rispuosi: «Madonna, sì devoto
com’ esser posso più, ringrazio lui
lo qual dal mortal mondo m’ha remoto.

I replied, "My Lady, as devoutly as I am able, I thank Him who has removed me from the mortal world."

Ma ditemi: che son li segni bui
di questo corpo, che là giuso in terra
fan di Cain favoleggiare altrui?».

But tell me: what are the dark marks upon this body, that down below on Earth make men speak fables of Cain?

Ella sorrise alquanto, e poi «S’elli erra
l’oppinïon», mi disse, «d’i mortali
dove chiave di senso non diserra,

She smiled slightly, then said to me, "If the opinion of mortals errs, where the key to meaning does not unlock,

certo non ti dovrien punger li strali
d’ammirazione omai, poi dietro ai sensi
vedi che la ragione ha corte l’ali.

surely, the arrows of amazementshouldn't prick you now, since you see how reason's wingsare clipped, behind the senses.

Ma dimmi quel che tu da te ne pensi».
E io: «Ciò che n’appar qua sù diverso
credo che fanno i corpi rari e densi».

"But tell me what *you* think of this," he said.And I replied: "I believe that what appears different up here is caused by bodies that are rarefied and dense."

Ed ella: «Certo assai vedrai sommerso
nel falso il creder tuo, se bene ascolti
l’argomentar ch’io li farò avverso.

And she said: "Surely you will see your belief is submerged in falsehood if you listen closely to the argument I will make against it.

La spera ottava vi dimostra molti
lumi, li quali e nel quale e nel quanto
notar si posson di diversi volti.

The eighth sphere reveals many lights to you, which can be noted in the where and the how much, in their various aspects.

Se raro e denso ciò facesser tanto,
una sola virtù sarebbe in tutti,
più e men distributa e altrettanto.

If this caused them to be so rare and dense, a single virtue would be in all, distributed in greater and lesser measure, and similarly.

Virtù diverse esser convegnon frutti
di princìpi formali, e quei, for ch’uno,
seguiterieno a tua ragion distrutti.

Various virtues must be the fruitsof formal principles, and those, except one,would be destroyed, following your reasoning.

Ancor, se raro fosse di quel bruno
cagion che tu dimandi, o d’oltre in parte
fora di sua materia sì digiuno

Even if that darkness were rarely the causeyou inquire about, or else its substancewere in some part so depleted,

esto pianeto, o, sì come comparte
lo grasso e ’l magro un corpo, così questo
nel suo volume cangerebbe carte.

this planet, or, yes, even as a body sharesfat and lean, so too would this onealter its properties in its volume.

Se ’l primo fosse, fora manifesto
ne l’eclissi del sol, per trasparere
lo lume come in altro raro ingesto.

If the first were so, it would be apparentin the sun's eclipse, for the light would pass through,as it does when entering another rare substance.

Questo non è: però è da vedere
de l’altro; e s’elli avvien ch’io l’altro cassi,
falsificato fia lo tuo parere.

This is not the case: but it remains to be seenabout the other; and if I should dismiss the other,your opinion will be proven false.

S’elli è che questo raro non trapassi,
esser conviene un termine da onde
lo suo contrario più passar non lassi;

If this rarity does not pass beyond, there must be a limit from which its opposite will no longer be allowed to cross;

e indi l’altrui raggio si rifonde
così come color torna per vetro
lo qual di retro a sé piombo nasconde.

and then the other light reflects itself, just as color returns through glass that hides lead behind it.

Or dirai tu ch’el si dimostra tetro
ivi lo raggio più che in altre parti,
per esser lì refratto più a retro.

Or will you say that it appears dark there, where the light is most intense, because it is reflected back more deeply?

Da questa instanza può deliberarti
esperïenza, se già mai la provi,
ch’esser suol fonte ai rivi di vostr’ arti.

From this condition experience can resolve you, if you ever test it, for it is accustomed to being the source of the streams of your arts.

Tre specchi prenderai; e i due rimovi
da te d’un modo, e l’altro, più rimosso,
tr’ambo li primi li occhi tuoi ritrovi.

Three mirrors you shall take; remove the first two from you in the same way, and let your eyes find the third, more distant, between the first two.

Rivolto ad essi, fa che dopo il dosso
ti stea un lume che i tre specchi accenda
e torni a te da tutti ripercosso.

Turning toward them, ensure that after the crest,a light remains to ignite the three mirrors,and returns to you, reflected from all.

Ben che nel quanto tanto non si stenda
la vista più lontana, lì vedrai
come convien ch’igualmente risplenda.

Although in the expanse, sight does not reach further, there you will see how it is right that it shine equally.

Or, come ai colpi de li caldi rai
de la neve riman nudo il suggetto
e dal colore e dal freddo primai,

Or, as the blows of the warm raysleave the subject of the snow bare,first from its color and then from its cold,

così rimaso te ne l’intelletto
voglio informar di luce sì vivace,
che ti tremolerà nel suo aspetto.

Thus, now remaining within your intellect, I wish to inform you with light so vibrant that it will tremble within its aspect.

Dentro dal ciel de la divina pace
si gira un corpo ne la cui virtute
l’esser di tutto suo contento giace.

From the heaven of divine peace, a body revolves, within whose power lies the being of all its contentment.

Lo ciel seguente, c’ha tante vedute,
quell’ esser parte per diverse essenze,
da lui distratte e da lui contenute.

The following heaven, which has so many sights, divides that being into diverse essences, detached from it, yet contained within it.

Li altri giron per varie differenze
le distinzion che dentro da sé hanno
dispongono a lor fini e lor semenze.

The other circles, through various differences, arrange the distinctions within themselves according to their ends and seeds.

Questi organi del mondo così vanno,
come tu vedi omai, di grado in grado,
che di sù prendono e di sotto fanno.

These instruments of the world proceed, as you now see, step by step, taking from above and creating below.

Riguarda bene omai sì com’ io vado
per questo loco al vero che disiri,
sì che poi sappi sol tener lo guado.

Now observe closely how I proceed through this place towards the truth that you desire, so that later you will know how to keep to the ford.

Lo moto e la virtù d’i santi giri,
come dal fabbro l’arte del martello,
da’ beati motor convien che spiri;

The motion and the power of the holy spheres, as the art of the hammer comes from the smith, must surely breathe forth from blessed movers;

e ’l ciel cui tanti lumi fanno bello,
de la mente profonda che lui volve
prende l’image e fassene suggello.

And the heaven, made beautiful by so many lights, takes its image and makes it a seal from the profound mind that moves it.

E come l’alma dentro a vostra polve
per differenti membra e conformate
a diverse potenze si risolve,

And as the soul within your dust,through differing limbs and forms,is resolved to diverse powers,

così l’intelligenza sua bontate
multiplicata per le stelle spiega,
girando sé sovra sua unitate.

Thus its intelligence, its goodness,multiplied by the stars, reveals itself,revolving around its own unity.

Virtù diversa fa diversa lega
col prezïoso corpo ch’ella avviva,
nel qual, sì come vita in voi, si lega.

Different virtue creates a different bondwith the precious body that it enlivens,in which, as life in you, it is bound.

Per la natura lieta onde deriva,
la virtù mista per lo corpo luce
come letizia per pupilla viva.

By its joyful nature, from which it originates, the mixed virtue shines through the body, as happiness does through a living pupil.

Da essa vien ciò che da luce a luce
par differente, non da denso e raro;
essa è formal principio che produce,

From it comes that which gives light to light,appearing different, not from dense and rare;it is the formal principle that creates,

conforme a sua bontà, lo turbo e ’l chiaro».

in accordance with His goodness, both the turbid and the clear».
